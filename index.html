Save point

Practical tools for school IT teams.

SaveByte is James Berryman’s small UK brand for the school apps he builds as a hobby. James is an IT engineer at a UK school, and the tools follow that work: devices to account for, repairs to move along, and a library desk that has to keep the queue moving.

CRASH looks after Chromebooks. Spyro looks after book loans. Both run in the browser, on Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, with pupils drawn from Arbor.
