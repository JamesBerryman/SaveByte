Save point

Practical tools for school IT teams.

SaveByte is a small UK developer that designs and builds apps for schools. They're made for the everyday jobs that keep a school running: keeping track of devices, getting repairs sorted, and helping the library desk keep the queue moving..

CRASH manages Chromebook allocation, repairs and storage. SPYRO runs the school library's book loans. Both work in the browser, are built on Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, and pull pupil details straight from your school's MIS.
