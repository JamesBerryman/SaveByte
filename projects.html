Projects

Two tools, built for the school day.

Both apps are Google Apps Script web apps sitting on Google Sheets. Pupil records come from an Arbor feed. There is no extra server to run. The CRASH pictures use fictional people and serials. The Spyro pictures are screenshots and show books only. On a phone, scroll each picture sideways to read it.

01

CRASH

Chromebook Repair, Asset and Storage Hub · Google Apps Script · Google Sheets · Arbor · Google Admin

CRASH is the Chromebook register for the IT team. It shows who a device is assigned to, whether it is out on loan, which trolley or office it lives in, and what is booked in for repair. Google Admin device sync keeps serials, models, status and the last sign-in up to date. Arbor supplies the pupil roll used when a machine is assigned.

Devices and repairs are limited to IT staff. Teachers get a separate lookup: search a pupil or a serial and see the device, the last sign-in, and whether it is in repair.

Key features

  • Search the register by serial, name or email.
  • Assign a Chromebook to a pupil, a member of staff, or a storage place such as the IT office or a trolley.
  • Mark a device as a loan, and return that loan to the IT office in one step.
  • Flag a machine when the last Google sign-in does not match the person it is assigned to.
  • Log repairs for screen, power, battery, hinge, keyboard, operating system, touchpad, Wi-Fi, water damage, and anything else.
  • Move a repair through new, awaiting collection, awaiting payment, third party, and resolved, with booked, repaired and resolved dates.
  • Open repair insights, and email a pupil’s tutor when a repair needs them.
  • Note devices and pupils that keep coming back for repair.
  • Show the last Google Admin and Arbor sync on the page, and keep a nightly backup.

Spyro

School library system · Google Apps Script · Google Sheets · Arbor

Spyro is the page the library desk works in. Staff search the catalogue, check a book out, and take it back by scanning the asset-tag sticker on that copy. The pupil list is pulled from Arbor, including leavers, so a book can still be traced after someone has left.

Lending and returning stay on the front page. The staff area — dashboard, insights, stock audit, adding books, the full catalogue, pupil history, overdue emails and saved reports — opens for the signed-in Google account.

Key features

  • Search by title, author, shelf or barcode, and filter to copies that are on the shelf.
  • Check out and return from large desk buttons, with a receipt left on screen and the cursor ready for the next scan.
  • Find a pupil by name, UPN, registration form, or the Arbor student ID printed on the library card.
  • Lend for a set number of days (14 unless the school changes it) and stop at a per-pupil limit, usually 3, with a staff override for one extra book.
  • Tie each loan and return to the asset tag on that copy, so a shared ISBN cannot clear the wrong book.
  • Renew from the on-loan list. A book that is already overdue has to come back first.
  • Add copies by looking up an ISBN, then scanning each asset tag. A stock audit can record that a sticker was seen without changing the loan.
  • Show the whole catalogue, pupil history, and leavers who still have books out.
  • Send overdue email as one message per family, and skip a book that was already emailed in the last week.
  • Scan with a USB scanner, or with the camera (including a photo when the live camera is blocked).
  • Save an overdue report and an insights report back to the spreadsheet, ready to print or save as PDF.
